Last Updated: 2026-10-17. Researched by two Claude Haiku 5.5 children (high effort); the Berkeley winter date was re-checked on commencement.berkeley.edu. "Unverified" marks figures with no primary source.

## Prices (Claire: $150/hr; $450 / 3 h, $600 / 4 h, $1200 / 8 h)

- Event photography, SF: $150 (Airbnb, "Candid moments by Johnny"), from $325/hr (Nico Hend, cliqora.com), from $400 (Chris Conner, Google listing). Median $325/hr. Bay Area low-end median $250/hr (eventective.com, Santa Clara list). No SF competitor publishes 3/4/8 h packages.
- Graduation, 1 h session: $150–$400 across seven listings, median $300 (Tyler Wang on Thumbtack $300 for 10 photos; Nat Dolan $280 mini, $400 for 1 h with 35 photos; Stanford Airbnb $200 for 45 min, $350 for a group of up to 4; Sean Phillips SF $350, snippet only). Snappr's SF averages ($324/h event) are unverified.

## Permits and business setup (checked 2026-10-17)

- UC Berkeley: commercial still photography on any campus property goes through the Campus Filming Program (film.berkeley.edu; contact Kyle Gibson, kyle.gibson@berkeley.edu, 510-495-5786), which issues a Film License Agreement and sets fees. Commercial shoots are fitted mainly into breaks (Winter Break Dec 2026–Jan 2027). The site does not say whether a small portrait session is exempt: ask the office.
- SF parks, including the Palace of Fine Arts: Rec and Park treats any paid photography as commercial and issues the permit; no application fee, at least five business days' notice, weekends discouraged (sfrecpark.org/428/Film-Video-and-Photo; permits 415-831-5500).
- San Francisco business registration is required within 30 days of starting business in the city (working or soliciting there on any 7 days a year), sole proprietors included. Online application at etaxstatement.sfgov.org/NewBusinessRegistration/application, then a DocuSign and a payment email; the payment confirmation carries the Business Account Number (BAN). Fee for the 2026–27 year (April–March) at $0–$100,000 SF gross receipts: $55 plus a $4 state fee; renewal each February. First Year Free does not cover home-based businesses (sftreasurer.org/registration, sftreasurer.org/business/first-year-free). A trade name other than her legal name needs a Fictitious Business Name filing with the County Clerk, which requires the BAN first.
- A California LLC owes the $800 annual franchise tax from its first year; the first-year waiver covered 2021–2023 formations only (secondary sources; confirm with the FTB). An LLC is not needed to take bookings.
