# San Francisco bookings market: events and graduations

Last Updated: 2026-10-17. Researched by two Claude Haiku 5.5 children (high effort); the Berkeley winter date was re-checked on commencement.berkeley.edu. "Unverified" marks figures with no primary source.

## Prices (Claire: $150/hr; $450 / 3 h, $600 / 4 h, $1200 / 8 h)

- Event photography, SF: $150 (Airbnb, "Candid moments by Johnny"), from $325/hr (Nico Hend, cliqora.com), from $400 (Chris Conner, Google listing). Median $325/hr. Bay Area low-end median $250/hr (eventective.com, Santa Clara list). No SF competitor publishes 3/4/8 h packages.
- Graduation, 1 h session: $150–$400 across seven listings, median $300 (Tyler Wang on Thumbtack $300 for 10 photos; Nat Dolan $280 mini, $400 for 1 h with 35 photos; Stanford Airbnb $200 for 45 min, $350 for a group of up to 4; Sean Phillips SF $350, snippet only). Snappr's SF averages ($324/h event) are unverified.

## Graduation calendar and campus rules

- Next: SJSU Dec 16–17, 2026 (tentative, sjsu.edu calendar); UC Berkeley Dec 19, 2026, 10 a.m., Haas Pavilion (commencement.berkeley.edu). Spring 2027: Berkeley May 15, SF State May 21 (Oracle Park), SJSU May 26–28, Stanford June 13. UCSF, USF, SCU, CCSF, CCA: not announced.
- Grads book about 2–3 months ahead (photographer blogs, not Bay Area data): December grads now, spring grads March–April.
- Berkeley: paid shoots on campus need a Campus Filming Program permit, about one month's lead time (film.berkeley.edu). Pro Pics shoots the ceremony and gives grads free photos. Official tag `#CalGrad`.
- Stanford: professional portrait shoots on the Main Quad and Memorial Court are not allowed; other professional filming needs written approval and insurance.
